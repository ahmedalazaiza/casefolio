## Create your first case study

Casefolio turns finished UI screens into a structured, publishable case study without starting from an empty canvas. Every project begins with a curated template maintained by the Casefolio team.

1. Open **Projects** and select **New project**.
2. Choose your case study type: **Mobile app**, **Website**, or **Mobile + Web**.
3. Pick a published template such as **Orbit** or **Frame** and preview its desktop and mobile layout.
4. Enter a project title and launch the constrained editor.

<Callout tone="tip">
Your Free workspace includes up to 2 projects so you can test the full workflow from slot upload to public link before upgrading.
</Callout>

## Fill content and screenshot slots

Inside the editor, select any section in the left outline to update headlines, metrics, role metadata and narrative paragraphs. Use the right **Screenshots** drawer to upload PNG, JPEG or WebP screens directly into aspect-ratio locked device slots.

- Required slots are highlighted before publishing so no empty frames slip onto your live portfolio.
- Every edit autosaves automatically, and you can create named checkpoints in **Version history** at any time.
