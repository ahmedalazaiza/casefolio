## Create your first case study

Casefolio turns finished UI screens into a structured, publishable case study — without staring at a blank canvas. Every project starts with a proven case study template crafted and maintained by the Casefolio team.

1. Open **Projects** and click **New project**.
2. Choose your project format: **Mobile app**, **Website**, or **Mobile + Web**.
3. Pick a template such as **Orbit**, **Cinema**, or **Northwind** and preview both desktop and mobile views.
4. Enter a working title and launch the editor.

<Callout tone="tip">
Your Free (\$0 / forever) workspace includes 1 active Case Study with a Live Shareable Link and PNG & PDF exports (with a subtle Casefolio watermark), so you can publish your first piece before upgrading to Pro for unlimited Case Studies.
</Callout>

## Fill story blocks and screenshot frames

Inside the editor, select any section in the left outline to update headlines, project metrics, role details, and narrative copy. Open the **Screenshots** drawer on the right to upload PNG, JPEG, or WebP files directly into aspect-ratio-locked device mockups.

- Required image slots are highlighted before publishing so unfinished frames never slip onto a live page.
- Every edit saves automatically, and you can capture named snapshots in **Version history** anytime.
