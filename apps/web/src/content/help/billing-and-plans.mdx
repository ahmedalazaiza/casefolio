## Plans and workspace limits

Casefolio offers three plans tailored to individual designers and collaborative studios:

- **Free:** Up to 2 projects, Free templates, public/unlisted pages and 5 watermarked PNG exports per month.
- **Pro:** Unlimited projects, all Free & Pro templates, unwatermarked PNG/PDF/Behance exports, badge-free public pages and full referrer analytics.
- **Team:** Everything in Pro plus 3+ collaborative studio seats (`Owner`, `Editor`, `Viewer`), shared studio brand kits and 10 GB upload storage.

## Invoices, VAT and payment methods

Payments are processed by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record. Paddle handles credit/debit cards, local tax/VAT calculation and downloadable tax invoices. Casefolio never stores raw card numbers.

<Callout tone="info">
Need to add a VAT ID, update your payment card or download past receipts? Go to **Settings → Billing** and open the customer billing portal.
</Callout>

## Cancelling or changing plans

You can switch between Monthly and Yearly billing or cancel your subscription anytime in **Settings → Billing**. When you cancel, Pro or Team features remain active until the end of your current paid period, and your projects stay safely stored in your account.
