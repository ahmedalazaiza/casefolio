## Plans and workspace limits

Casefolio separates **Personal Workspaces** and **Team Workspaces** cleanly so joining a team never affects or exposes your personal work. Annual billing saves **20% (2 months free)** across all paid tiers:

- **Free ($0 / forever):** Default onboarding plan. Includes 1 personal workspace (1 seat), up to **1 active Case Study**, standard templates, basic customization, **PNG & PDF export with subtle Casefolio watermark**, and a Live Shareable Link.
- **Pro ($9.99/mo, or $7.99/mo billed annually at $95.88/yr):** Everything in Free, plus **Unlimited Case Studies**, all **Pro & Premium** templates, full customization (custom fonts, color palettes, custom sections), high-resolution **PNG, PDF & Behance exports without watermark**, and basic view analytics on live links.
- **Team ($6.99/seat/mo, or $5.59/seat/mo billed annually at $67.08/seat/yr — 2 to 10 seats):** Dynamic per-seat plan (`seats × seat_price`) for collaborative studios. Everything in Pro, plus a Shared Team Workspace with role-based permissions (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Member`), **Unlimited** shared Case Studies, Shared Brand Kit (team logos, colors, reusable presets), and real-time review workflows.
- **Plus ($99/mo flat, or $79/mo billed annually at $948/yr — up to 20 seats included):** Predictable flat-rate agency plan with no per-seat math. Everything in Team, plus up to **20 team seats**, **Custom Domain** (`work.youragency.com`), complete **White-Labeling** (full removal of Casefolio branding from live links, OpenGraph cards, and PDF metadata), and Priority 24/7 support & dedicated onboarding.

## Seat changes on Casefolio Team & Plus

- **Adjusting Team seats (`2–10` seats):** Workspace Owners preview the exact prorated charge before confirming seat increases, which activate immediately once payment succeeds. Seat decreases can be scheduled for the next renewal down to the number of occupied seats (`>= 2`).
- **Growing beyond 10 seats:** Attempting to invite an 11th teammate on **Team** prompts a seamless upgrade to **Casefolio Plus**, unlocking up to **20 included seats**, Custom Domain, and White-Labeling.

## Invoices, VAT and payment methods

Payments are processed by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record. Paddle handles credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, local tax/VAT calculation, and downloadable tax invoices. Casefolio never stores raw card numbers.

<Callout tone="info">
Need to add a VAT ID, update your payment card, or download past receipts? Go to **Settings → Billing** and open the customer billing portal.
</Callout>

## Past-due grace, non-destructive downgrades, and member recovery

- **Past-due grace (7 days):** Failed renewals enter a 7-day grace window from the first failed charge before workspaces become read-only.
- **Downgrade protection (Paid → Free):** If you downgrade to Free with more than 1 Case Study, **none of your Case Studies are deleted**. Extra Case Studies are safely unpublished and locked for editing while you choose **1 active Case Study** to keep live and editable (which you can switch anytime in **Settings → Billing** or on your Dashboard until you upgrade again).
- **Departed member recovery (≥90 days):** If a member leaves or is removed from a Team or Plus workspace, their private team spaces stay hidden from Owners/Admins and remain accessible in **Settings → Recovery** for at least 90 days to copy into their Personal Workspace or export as JSON.
