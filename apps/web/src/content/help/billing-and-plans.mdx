## Plans and workspace limits

Casefolio cleanly separates **Personal Workspaces** and **Shared Team Workspaces** so joining a studio or agency never exposes or alters your personal portfolio. Annual billing includes **2 months free** on every paid plan:

- **Free (\$0 / forever):** Included automatically at signup. Includes 1 personal workspace (1 user), **1 one-time lifetime Case Study credit** (full A-to-Z builder, starter templates, and archiving/unarchiving included; deleting a project does not grant a new credit), `1×` **PNG & PDF exports** (with a subtle Casefolio watermark), and a shareable auto-generated public link (`/p/...`).
- **Pro (\$7.99/mo, or \$6.66/mo billed annually at \$79.90/yr — 2 months free):** Everything in Free, plus up to **10 active Case Studies** (delete or archive anytime to rotate projects), **Custom Case Study URLs** (`/p/your-custom-slug`), all **Pro & Premium** templates, full customization (custom fonts, color palettes, custom sections), watermark-free `2×` Retina **PNG, PDF & Behance 1400px exports**, and 90-day view analytics on live links.
- **Teams (\$5.99/seat/mo, or \$4.99/seat/mo billed annually at \$59.90/seat/yr — 2 months free, 2 to 25 seats):** Flexible per-seat billing (`seats × seat_price`) for product design teams, studios, and agencies. Includes everything in Pro, plus a Shared Team Workspace, **Unlimited** shared Case Studies, a Team Dashboard (invite members by email, track invite status, and manage Owner / Admin / Member roles), a Shared Brand Kit (team logos, colors, reusable sections), `10 GB/seat` pooled storage, and 365-day view analytics.

## Seat changes on Casefolio Teams

- **Adjusting Teams seats (`2–25` seats):** Workspace Owners preview the exact prorated charge before confirming additional seats, which activate immediately once payment succeeds. Seat reductions can be scheduled for the next renewal down to the number of occupied seats (`>= 2`).
- **Growing past 25 seats:** Teams needing **26+ seats** can reach out to Casefolio enterprise support from **Settings → Billing** or the Pricing page for custom volume capacity and invoicing.

## Invoices, VAT, and payment methods

All payments are handled securely by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record. Paddle supports major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, local sales tax/VAT calculation, and downloadable tax invoices. Casefolio never sees or stores raw payment card numbers.

<Callout tone="info">
Need to add a VAT ID, update your payment method, or download past receipts? Visit **Settings → Billing** to open the customer billing portal.
</Callout>

## Past-due grace period, downgrade protection, and member recovery

- **Past-due grace period (7 days):** Failed renewal charges enter a 7-day grace window from the first failed charge before workspaces become read-only.
- **Non-destructive downgrades (Paid → Free):** If you downgrade to Free with more than 1 Case Study, **none of your work is deleted**. Additional Case Studies are unpublished and safely locked for editing while you select **1 active Case Study** to keep live and editable — switch anytime in **Settings → Billing** or on your Dashboard until you upgrade again.
- **Departed member recovery (≥90 days):** When a teammate leaves or is removed from a Teams workspace, their private drafts stay hidden from Owners and Admins and remain accessible in **Settings → Recovery** for at least 90 days to copy into a Personal Workspace or export as JSON.
