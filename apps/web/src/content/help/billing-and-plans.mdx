## Plans and workspace limits

Casefolio separates **Personal Workspaces** and **Team Workspaces** cleanly so joining a team never affects or exposes your personal work:

- **Free ($0/mo):** 1 personal workspace (1 person), up to **2 active projects** (1 published, 10 retained), 3 Starter templates, **5 branded 1x PNG exports/mo** (1,440 px), 250 MB storage, 500 MB uploads/mo, 10 MB max file, 1 GB/mo public bandwidth, and 7-day analytics & history.
- **Pro ($12/mo or $120/yr — $10/mo):** 1 personal workspace (1 person), up to **20 active projects** (10 published, 100 retained), all eligible published templates, **60 exports/mo** across clean 1x/2x PNG (2,880 px), A4 PDF, and Behance 1,400w slices, removable branding, 5 GB storage, 10 GB uploads/mo, 25 MB max file, up to 5 private spaces, 90-day analytics, and 30-day history.
- **Teams ($18 × S/mo or $180 × S/yr — $15 × S/mo, S = 1–25 seats):** 1 team workspace where every account (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Member`) occupies 1 paid seat. Includes **20 × S active projects** (10 × S published, 100 × S retained), **5 private spaces per member** (hidden from Owners & Admins by default) plus **10 × S shared spaces**, **100 × S exports/mo**, 10 GB × S storage, 20 GB × S uploads/mo, 25 GB × S bandwidth/mo, 365-day analytics, and 90-day recoverable history.

## Seat changes on Casefolio Teams

- **Adding seats (`S = 1–25`):** Workspace Owners preview the exact prorated charge before confirming. Additional seats activate immediately once payment succeeds. Above 25 seats, contact support for a capacity review.
- **Decreasing seats:** Workspace Owners can schedule a lower renewal seat count down to the number of currently occupied seats (active members + pending 7-day invitations). Your current paid capacity remains active until the next billing renewal.

## Invoices, VAT and payment methods

Payments are processed by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record. Paddle handles credit/debit cards, local tax/VAT calculation, and downloadable tax invoices. Casefolio never stores raw card numbers.

<Callout tone="info">
Need to add a VAT ID, update your payment card, or download past receipts? Go to **Settings → Billing** and open the customer billing portal.
</Callout>

## Past-due grace, downgrades, and member recovery

- **Past-due grace (7 days):** Failed renewals enter a 7-day grace window from the first failed charge before workspaces become read-only.
- **Pro → Free downgrade:** After your paid period ends, choose 1 published page to keep live; additional published pages unpublish safely without deleting your work.
- **Departed member recovery (≥90 days):** If a member leaves or is removed from a Team workspace, their private team spaces stay hidden from Owners/Admins and remain accessible in **Settings → Recovery** for at least 90 days to copy into their Personal Workspace or export as JSON.
