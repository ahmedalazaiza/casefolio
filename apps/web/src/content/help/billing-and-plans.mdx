## Plans and workspace limits

Casefolio cleanly separates **Personal Workspaces** and **Shared Team Workspaces** so joining a studio or agency never exposes or alters your personal portfolio. Annual billing saves **20% (2 months free)** on every paid plan:

- **Free (\$0 / forever):** Included automatically at signup. Includes 1 personal workspace (1 user), up to **1 active Case Study**, standard templates, essential styling, **PNG & PDF exports with a subtle Casefolio watermark**, and a Live Shareable Link.
- **Pro (\$9.99/mo, or \$7.99/mo billed annually at \$95.88/yr):** Everything in Free, plus **Unlimited Case Studies**, all **Pro & Premium** templates, full customization (custom fonts, color palettes, custom sections), watermark-free high-res **PNG, PDF & Behance 1400px exports**, and 90-day view analytics on live links.
- **Team (\$6.99/seat/mo, or \$5.59/seat/mo billed annually at \$67.08/seat/yr — 2 to 10 seats):** Flexible per-seat billing (`seats × seat_price`) for product design teams and studios. Includes everything in Pro, plus a Shared Team Workspace with role-based permissions, **Unlimited** shared Case Studies, a Shared Brand Kit (team logos, colors, reusable sections), and real-time commenting & review workflows.
- **Plus (\$99/mo flat, or \$79/mo billed annually at \$948/yr — up to 20 seats included):** Flat-rate agency plan with no per-seat math. Includes everything in Team, plus up to **20 team seats**, a **Custom Domain** (`work.youragency.com`), complete **White-Labeling** (full removal of Casefolio branding across live links, OpenGraph cards, and PDF metadata), and Priority 24/7 support & dedicated onboarding.

## Seat changes on Casefolio Team & Plus

- **Adjusting Team seats (`2–10` seats):** Workspace Owners preview the exact prorated charge before confirming additional seats, which activate immediately once payment succeeds. Seat reductions can be scheduled for the next renewal down to the number of occupied seats (`>= 2`).
- **Growing past 10 seats:** Inviting an 11th teammate on **Team** prompts a smooth upgrade to **Casefolio Plus**, unlocking up to **20 included seats**, Custom Domain support, and complete White-Labeling.

## Invoices, VAT, and payment methods

All payments are handled securely by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record. Paddle supports major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, local sales tax/VAT calculation, and downloadable tax invoices. Casefolio never sees or stores raw payment card numbers.

<Callout tone="info">
Need to add a VAT ID, update your payment method, or download past receipts? Visit **Settings → Billing** to open the customer billing portal.
</Callout>

## Past-due grace period, downgrade protection, and member recovery

- **Past-due grace period (7 days):** Failed renewal charges enter a 7-day grace window from the first failed charge before workspaces become read-only.
- **Non-destructive downgrades (Paid → Free):** If you downgrade to Free with more than 1 Case Study, **none of your work is deleted**. Additional Case Studies are unpublished and safely locked for editing while you select **1 active Case Study** to keep live and editable — switch anytime in **Settings → Billing** or on your Dashboard until you upgrade again.
- **Departed member recovery (≥90 days):** When a teammate leaves or is removed from a Team or Plus workspace, their private drafts stay hidden from Owners and Admins and remain accessible in **Settings → Recovery** for at least 90 days to copy into a Personal Workspace or export as JSON.
