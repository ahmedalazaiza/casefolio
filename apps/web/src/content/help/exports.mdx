## Pixel-identical PNG, PDF and Behance exports

Casefolio renders exports through the exact same layout engine as your live preview, ensuring typography, device frames and theme tokens match 1:1.

- **PNG (Long image):** Full-page Stitch PNG ideal for quick sharing and archive backups. Available on **Free** (`1×` 1,440 px with subtle Casefolio watermark) and watermark-free at `1×` & `2×` retina (`2,880 px`) on **Pro**, **Team**, and **Plus**.
- **PDF (Paged A4 document):** Print-ready multi-page PDF portfolio document. Available on **Free** (with subtle Casefolio watermark), watermark-free on **Pro** & **Team**, and completely **White-Labeled** (zero Casefolio strings in PDF title/producer metadata) on **Plus**.
- **Behance 1400px slices (ZIP):** Automatically slices long case studies into crisp `1400px`-wide images packaged in a single ZIP archive for Behance projects on **Pro**, **Team**, and **Plus**.

<Callout tone="tip">
Track active jobs, retry failed renders and re-download recent files anytime from the **Exports** page in your workspace sidebar.
</Callout>

## Download link expiry

Signed download URLs remain active for 7 days after an export finishes. If an older export expires, open your project or **Exports** history and generate a fresh render in seconds.
