## Export your full case study to Layerat, PNG & PDF

Casefolio generates exports using the exact same rendering engine as your live preview, so fonts, device frames, spacing, and brand colors match 1:1.

- **Layerat Full Case Study Pack (ZIP):** Exports your complete case study into seamless `1400px`-wide high-resolution vertical panels packaged in a ready-to-upload ZIP archive so you can publish directly to **Layerat** on **Pro** and **Teams**.
- **PNG (Long scroll image):** Full-page PNG export ideal for portfolio archives and quick client shares. Available on **Free** (`1×` 1,440 px with a subtle Casefolio watermark) and watermark-free at `1×` & `2×` Retina (`2,880 px`) on **Pro** and **Teams**.
- **PDF (Paged A4 document):** Print-ready multi-page A4 portfolio deck for hiring managers and clients. Available on **Free** (with a subtle Casefolio watermark) and completely watermark-free on **Pro** and **Teams**.

<Callout tone="tip">
Monitor active jobs, retry failed renders, and re-download recent files anytime from the **Exports** page in your workspace sidebar.
</Callout>

## Download link expiry

Signed download links remain valid for 7 days after each export completes. When an older link expires, simply open your project or **Exports** history to generate a fresh file in seconds.
