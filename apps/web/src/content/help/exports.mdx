## Pixel-identical PNG, PDF and Behance exports

Casefolio generates exports using the exact same rendering engine as your live preview, so fonts, device frames, spacing, and brand colors match 1:1.

- **PNG (Long scroll image):** Full-page PNG export ideal for portfolio archives and quick client shares. Available on **Free** (`1×` 1,440 px with a subtle Casefolio watermark) and watermark-free at `1×` & `2×` retina (`2,880 px`) on **Pro**, **Team**, and **Plus**.
- **PDF (Paged A4 document):** Print-ready multi-page A4 portfolio deck for hiring managers and clients. Available on **Free** (with subtle Casefolio watermark), watermark-free on **Pro** & **Team**, and fully **White-Labeled** (no Casefolio strings in PDF title or producer metadata) on **Plus**.
- **Behance 1400px slices (ZIP):** Automatically splits tall case studies into crisp `1400px`-wide images bundled into a ready-to-upload ZIP archive on **Pro**, **Team**, and **Plus**.

<Callout tone="tip">
Monitor active jobs, retry failed renders, and re-download recent files anytime from the **Exports** page in your workspace sidebar.
</Callout>

## Download link expiry

Signed download links remain valid for 7 days after each export completes. When an older link expires, simply open your project or **Exports** history to generate a fresh file in seconds.
