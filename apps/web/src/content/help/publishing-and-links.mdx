## Publish a public or unlisted case study

Click **Publish** in the top bar of the editor to run the pre-publish slot check and publish a live snapshot at `casefolio.app/p/...`.

- **Public:** Indexed with clean canonical metadata and dynamic OpenGraph preview cards for LinkedIn, X, and Slack.
- **Unlisted:** Accessible only to recipients with your direct link and marked `noindex` for search engines — ideal for NDA or recruiter-only reviews.
- **Draft:** Takes the live URL offline immediately (`404`) while keeping every edit in your workspace intact.

## Auto-generated Free links & custom slugs on Pro and Teams

- **Free:** Publishes your Case Study immediately on a permanent auto-generated shareable link (`casefolio.app/p/project-name-id`).
- **Pro & Teams:** Unlock **Custom Case Study URLs** (`casefolio.app/p/your-custom-slug`) with real-time slug availability checks inside **Share & Publish**, plus the option to hide the “Made with Casefolio” footer badge on live shareable links.

<Callout tone="warning">
Renaming a project from your Dashboard does not alter its published URL slug unless you change the slug inside Share settings — keeping links on your resume and job applications safe from breaking.
</Callout>

## Reading privacy-first analytics

Published case studies collect privacy-safe view analytics without third-party tracking cookies. **Pro** (90-day retention) and **Teams** (365-day retention) workspaces unlock unique visitor counts, daily traffic trends, top referrers (like LinkedIn or Dribbble), and country breakdowns inside the **Analytics** tab.
