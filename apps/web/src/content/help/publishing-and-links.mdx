## Publish a public or unlisted page

Click **Publish** in the top bar of the editor to run the pre-publish slot check and push a clean snapshot live at `casefolio.app/p/your-slug` (or on your agency’s **Custom Domain** on **Casefolio Plus**).

- **Public:** Indexed with canonical metadata and dynamic OpenGraph social cards for LinkedIn, X and Slack.
- **Unlisted:** Accessible only to people with the direct link and marked `noindex` for search engines.
- **Draft:** Immediately takes the public URL offline (`404`) while keeping your editor workspace intact.

## Custom slugs, Custom Domains & White-Labeling

You can customise your project’s URL slug inside the **Share & Publish** panel. Casefolio validates uniqueness and reserves system paths automatically.

- **Pro & Team:** Hide the “Made with Casefolio” footer badge on live shareable links.
- **Plus ($99/mo or $79/mo billed annually):** Connect a **Custom Domain** (e.g. `work.youragency.com`) in **Settings → Billing** and enable complete **White-Labeling** across live links, OpenGraph cards, and PDF metadata.

<Callout tone="warning">
Renaming a project from your dashboard does not change its public slug unless you explicitly update the slug in Share settings, preventing broken links on resumes and job applications.
</Callout>

## Reading analytics

Every published case study records privacy-friendly page views. **Pro**, **Team**, and **Plus** workspaces unlock unique visitor counts, daily view trends, top referrers (such as LinkedIn or Dribbble) and country breakdowns in the **Analytics** tab.
