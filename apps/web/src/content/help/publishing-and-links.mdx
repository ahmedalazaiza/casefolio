## Publish a public or unlisted case study

Click **Publish** in the top bar of the editor to run the pre-publish slot check and publish a live snapshot at `casefolio.app/p/your-slug` (or on your own **Custom Domain** with **Casefolio Plus**).

- **Public:** Indexed with clean canonical metadata and dynamic OpenGraph preview cards for LinkedIn, X, and Slack.
- **Unlisted:** Accessible only to recipients with your direct link and marked `noindex` for search engines — ideal for NDA or recruiter-only reviews.
- **Draft:** Takes the live URL offline immediately (`404`) while keeping every edit in your workspace intact.

## Custom slugs, Custom Domains & White-Labeling

Customize your URL slug anytime inside the **Share & Publish** panel. Casefolio validates slug uniqueness and reserves system paths automatically.

- **Pro & Team:** Hide the “Made with Casefolio” footer badge on live shareable links.
- **Plus (\$99/mo or \$79/mo billed annually):** Connect a **Custom Domain** (such as `work.youragency.com`) in **Settings → Billing** and enable complete **White-Labeling** across live links, OpenGraph cards, and PDF metadata.

<Callout tone="warning">
Renaming a project from your Dashboard does not alter its published URL slug unless you change the slug inside Share settings — keeping links on your resume and job applications safe from breaking.
</Callout>

## Reading privacy-first analytics

Published case studies collect privacy-safe view analytics without third-party tracking cookies. **Pro**, **Team**, and **Plus** workspaces unlock unique visitor counts, daily traffic trends, top referrers (like LinkedIn or Dribbble), and country breakdowns inside the **Analytics** tab.
