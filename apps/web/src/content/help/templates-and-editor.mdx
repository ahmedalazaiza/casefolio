## Constrained editing by design

Casefolio templates intentionally lock grid spacing, responsive breakpoints and visual hierarchy so your portfolio stays crisp on every screen size. Instead of dragging raw rectangles, you edit structured fields and semantic theme tokens.

## Customising colours and fonts

Open the **Style** tab in the editor to personalise your template’s design tokens:

- **Colour tokens:** Primary accent, secondary accent, canvas background and foreground text.
- **Curated font pairs:** Choose from licensed display and body font pairings tuned for editorial readability.
- **Reset anytime:** Click **Reset to template defaults** to restore the original palette in one click.

<Callout tone="info">
On the Team plan, your studio can save shared brand colours, typography presets and a studio logo inside **Workspace Settings → Brand Kit**.
</Callout>

## Cropping screenshots and managing sections

Every image slot specifies its target device ratio (for example `9:19.5` for modern phones or `16:10` for desktop browsers). When you upload or replace an image, the non-destructive cropper stores normalised crop coordinates while preserving your original high-resolution upload.

- Toggle optional sections such as **Testimonials** or **Before / After** on and off when supported by the template version.
- When the Casefolio team ships a new version of a template, an **Upgrade template** banner lets you preview and migrate safely with automatic rollback history.
