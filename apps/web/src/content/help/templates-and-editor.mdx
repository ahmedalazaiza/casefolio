## Structured editing by design

Casefolio templates lock grid spacing, responsive breakpoints, and typographic hierarchy so your portfolio looks sharp on every viewport. Instead of nudging raw rectangles around a freeform canvas, you refine structured storytelling blocks and brand styles.

## Customizing colors and fonts

Open the **Style** tab in the editor to personalize your template’s visual system:

- **Brand colors:** Adjust primary accent, secondary highlight, canvas background, and foreground text colors — with built-in contrast checks.
- **Curated font pairs:** Switch between paired display and body fonts tuned for editorial legibility.
- **Reset anytime:** Click **Reset to template defaults** to restore the original styling in one click.

<Callout tone="info">
On the **Team** and **Plus** plans, your studio can save shared brand colors, typography presets, and a studio logo inside **Shared Brand Kit**.
</Callout>

## Cropping screenshots and managing sections

Every image frame specifies its target device aspect ratio (such as `9:19.5` for modern smartphones or `16:10` for desktop browsers). When you upload or replace a screen, the non-destructive cropper stores normalized crop coordinates while preserving your original high-resolution source file.

- Show, hide, reorder, or duplicate optional sections such as **Testimonials** or **Before / After** when supported by your template version.
- When the Casefolio team releases an updated template version, an **Upgrade template** banner lets you preview changes and migrate safely with automatic rollback history.
